Data & metric definitions

This proposal uses simulated campaign data, not live integrations. Sales revenue = product price × sales; creator earnings = 15% of sales revenue. ROI = (commissions - ad spend) / ad spend. Return multiple = commissions / ad spend. Engagement = (likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach. CTR = clicks / impressions. Reach is summed across campaigns and may count the same person more than once. Comparisons use the immediately preceding period of equal length. Checkout completion = sales / checkouts. MRR, churn, returning customers, NPS, CSAT, qualified leads, and lead time are not tracked without subscription, customer, survey, or CRM data. All sales are assumed attributed and non-refunded.
